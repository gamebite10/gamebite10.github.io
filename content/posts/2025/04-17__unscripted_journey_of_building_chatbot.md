Hello and welcome to our newest project [TurboHey](https://turbohey.com/chatbot-builder)!

Today marks the official beginning of a project we've been wanting to tackle for a long time: building a custom chatbot from the ground up. This first post is all about setting the stage and clarifying our central goal: making conversational AI easy and accessible for everyday business.

Our agent isn't just about automation—it's about seamless operation. The core features we're building around are:

Hybrid Agent Support: We want to make it easy for people and AI agents to work together. If the bot can't handle a request, a human agent can easily step in, ensuring smooth customer service.

Serverless & Scalable: The entire project is being built on a fully serverless architecture. This allows for massive scalability and eliminates the headache of infrastructure maintenance.

State Management: Crucially, the system is designed to maintain state and sessions easily. This makes it ideal for businesses like restaurants and repetitive service operations where customers often ask follow-up questions or need to reference previous interactions.

We've just finished setting up the basic repository and defining the core architecture—a thrilling mix of excitement and mild terror! Wish us luck!
